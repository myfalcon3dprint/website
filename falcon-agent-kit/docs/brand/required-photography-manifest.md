## Production Studio Photography Guidelines
1. **Lighting:** Single primary softbox at 45° with warm reflector fill (3200K–4000K).
2. **Background:** Warm neutral matte background paper or stone slab (`#FAFAF7` / `#F1F1EC`).
3. **Texture:** FDM/SLA layer fidelity and physical material characteristics must be visible without artificial over-smoothing.
4. **Honesty:** No false certifications or simulated functions (e.g., miniature brass cookware must carry explicit decorative-only notice).
